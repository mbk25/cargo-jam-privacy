Cargo Jam Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

English Türkçe

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Cargo Jam Puzzle (the “App”; Android package com.cargojam.cargo_jam_puzzle, iOS bundle com.cargojam.cargoJamPuzzle) handles information.

In short: the App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The App shows ads from Google AdMob; when it does, Google collects device information such as your advertising ID and IP address, as described in section 3.

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

The App is developed and published by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the processing described here:

  • Name: Muhammed Bilal Köseoğlu
  • Address: Yenidoğan Mah., Şehit Komiser Günaydın Sok. No: 144, Zeytinburnu / İstanbul, Türkiye
  • Email: mbkapp25@gmail.com

Google LLC (and, for users in the EEA/UK, Google Ireland Limited) provides the advertising service and decides independently how it uses the data it collects; see section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following game data only on your device (in the device's app storage):

  • Your level progress and the pictures you have unlocked;
  • Your in-game coin balance, hearts (lives) and their refill timer, and boosters;
  • Daily counters for optional reward videos and speed-ups, and whether you finished the tutorial;
  • A count of cleared levels, used only to space out full-screen ads;
  • Your settings (music, sound and vibration on/off).

This data is not sent to us or to any server we run, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings. Depending on your device's backup settings, your operating system may include it in your own device backup. In-game coins, hearts and boosters have no real-world monetary value.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The App shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus (for example an extra bay, double coins, a booster or a speed-up). They are always optional.
  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads between levels. None are shown on the first levels; after that, one may be shown after every second level you clear.

When the App starts and when ads are loaded or shown, the App connects to Google, and Google and its partners automatically collect and process information such as:

DataPurpose
Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, where permitted, personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, regional compliance
Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device, analytics, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic dataAd measurement, reporting and fraud prevention

On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track you, so it cannot read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

We do not receive this information about you individually; AdMob gives us only aggregated reports (for example how many ads were shown). Google collects it directly and handles it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob – personalised and non-personalised ads

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): in these regions the App shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised (limited) ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • Turkey: on a device set to Turkish or to the Turkey region, the App shows its own KVKK consent screen before any ad is requested; until you answer, no ad is requested. If you accept, Google may show personalised ads. If you decline, ads are requested as non-personalised; your IP address and device information are still sent to Google so that the ad can be shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • Other countries: the App does not show a consent form, and Google may show personalised ads. You can turn off personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you may opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising through the device settings above.
  • You never have to watch a rewarded ad to keep playing.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • Game data on your device – to run the game and keep your progress. This data never leaves your device through the App.
  • Advertising data collected by Google – to fund the free App by showing ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland this relies on the consent you give in Google's consent form. For users in Turkey, personalised ads and the transfer of this data abroad rely on the explicit consent you give on the App's KVKK screen (KVKK Art. 5(1) and Art. 9). 5. Other services the App connects to

  • Google Play in-app updates (Android): when the App opens, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play under Google's terms.
  • This policy: the “Privacy Policy” link in the App's settings opens this page in your browser. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when you open it.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been processed through the App, please contact us and we will help.

7. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location. It uses internet access and, on Android, the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) only for the ad purposes in section 3.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data; we hold no copy of it. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept for the periods set out in Google's retention policy; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

9. Security

We run no servers or databases for the App, so no personal information about you is stored on our systems. Information processed by Google is protected according to Google's security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. International transfers

The advertising data described in section 3 is processed by Google in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy and data processing terms.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, “KVKK”), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or wrong, and for it to be deleted;
  • ask for corrections or deletions to be notified to the third parties it was transferred to;
  • object to a result against you arising only from automated analysis;
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing;
  • withdraw consent and object to processing (GDPR), and complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Because we hold no personal data about you, data collected by Google for advertising is best managed through your device and Google account settings and Google's own request tools. You can still send us any request in writing to the postal address in section 1 or by email to mbkapp25@gmail.com; we will reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's store listing or in the App.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: mbkapp25@gmail.com
